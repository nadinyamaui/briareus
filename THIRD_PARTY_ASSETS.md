## Optional local asset

`public/assets/overwater_resort_hut.glb` is intentionally excluded from this
repository because its commercial marketplace license does not permit
redistribution of the source model. A user who has obtained their own suitable
license may place the model at that path locally. Briareus falls back to its
procedural bungalow when the file is absent.
